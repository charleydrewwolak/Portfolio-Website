In Spring 2024, Airbnb x Verb teamed up with Bednark to unveil a captivating new Icons experience series. The mission of Airbnb Icons is to celebrate 'IRL' with guests. Among the two Icon experiences brought to life by Bednark was the X-Mansion. By employing techniques usually reserved for film and TV, Bednark transformed the interior of this real mansion into an immersive environment that makes guests feel as though they’ve stepped into a 2D comic book.

Bednark X Verb
